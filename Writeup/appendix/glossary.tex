This glossary summarises the terminology and visual cues used consistently
throughout the pipeline and benchmark figures. The methodology chapter defines
the corresponding procedures in detail.

\section*{Key Terms}

{\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.28\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
  \midrule
  \textbf{Scenario} &
    The fixed application task and OpenAPI contract exercised by the
    benchmark. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Framework} &
    The implementation language/library stack and evaluation harness used to
    build, deploy, and measure a candidate. \\
  \textbf{Model configuration} &
    The assessed LLM together with generation settings such as temperature
    and budget. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Workload} &
    The verified Locust script together with the load profile that controls
    offered traffic during a benchmark. \\
  \textbf{Task} &
    One point of the experimental design, a
    (scenario, framework, model) tuple; 63 in total
    (Section~\ref{sec:methods-workflow}). \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Sample} &
    One independent execution trajectory of a task; here $n=1$ per task. \\
  \textbf{Experiment} &
    The execution and evaluation settings for a sample, i.e.\ one
    refinement trajectory. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Iteration} &
    One pass through the refinement pipeline. The baseline is
    iteration~\texttt{000}; refinement iterations run
    \texttt{001}--\texttt{010}. \\
  \textbf{Baseline} &
    The iteration~\texttt{000} that generates both the code image and the
    deployment specification for the first candidate. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Candidate} &
    The deployable pair of a validated container image and a validated
    \texttt{spec.yaml}. \\
  \textbf{Sustained goodput} &
    The primary metric: the best settled rate of \emph{successful} HTTP
    responses the refine phase can hold just short of saturation
    (Section~\ref{sec:methods-load-profile}). \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Goodput} &
    Successful HTTP responses per second at the currently offered load;
    sustained goodput is the selected settled value used to score a
    candidate. \\
  \textbf{Warm-up / health gate} &
    The fixed initial load period that checks active users, observed
    requests, and failure rate before adaptive probing begins. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Dead / unhealthy baseline} &
    A baseline that reports sustained goodput~$0$, having failed the
    warm-up or recovery health gate (Section~\ref{sec:results-rq1}). \\
  \textbf{Capacity knee} &
    The offered-load level past which a deployment tends to collapse
    rather than merely slow down. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Explore / recovery / refine} &
    The three active phases of the adaptive load profile after warm-up
    (Section~\ref{sec:methods-load-profile}). \\
  \textbf{Failure routing} &
    The stage-specific policy that sends a failure record back to the
    responsible regeneration or decision stage. \\
  \rowcolor{gray!12}
  \textbf{Latest successful-\allowbreak benchmark reference} &
    The most recent iteration with a completed benchmark, carried forward
    as the feedback reference point (Section~\ref{sec:methods-outcome}). \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\par}

    configured load profile. \\

    \textbf{Feedback report} &

    feedback report. \\

  \iconcell{\giconone{colored/failure-record}} &
    \textbf{Failure record} &
    Structured evidence from an unsuccessful stage, used for failure routing
    and, where appropriate, repair. \\
