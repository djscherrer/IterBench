This glossary summarises the terminology and visual cues used consistently
throughout the pipeline and benchmark figures. The methodology chapter defines
the corresponding procedures in detail.

{\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.05}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.31\linewidth}X@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Term and diagram cue} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
  \midrule
  \textbf{LLM agent}\newline
    \emph{Agent glyph; speech bubbles indicate a continuing conversation} &
    The assessed model or an offline authoring agent. Speech bubbles mean
    that the agent retains its preceding conversation context. \\
  \textbf{Scenario contract}\newline
    \emph{Light bulb and \texttt{API}} &
    The scenario idea together with its OpenAPI contract; the fixed
    application task provided to the benchmark. \\
  \textbf{Functional-test suite}\newline
    \emph{Checklist} &
    Executable API tests used to validate generated application code. \\
  \textbf{Container image}\newline
    \emph{Docker glyph} &
    The validated package containing the generated application and its
    runtime dependencies. \\
  \textbf{Deployment specification}\newline
    \emph{Kubernetes glyph} &
    The agent-proposed deployment configuration, statically validated by the
    framework before deployment. \\
  \textbf{Candidate}\newline
    \emph{Container image plus deployment specification} &
    The deployable pair of the latest validated image and deployment
    specification. \\
  \textbf{Locust workload}\newline
    \emph{Locust glyph} &
    The generated load-test script that exercises the scenario under the
    configured load profile. \\
  \textbf{Feedback report}\newline
    \emph{Blue report glyph} &
    The completed benchmark's load-test results and system-utilisation
    diagnostics, used to inform the next refinement. \\
  \textbf{Failure record}\newline
    \emph{Red failure glyph} &
    Structured evidence from an unsuccessful stage, used for failure routing
    and, where appropriate, repair. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
