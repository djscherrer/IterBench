This appendix lists exact parameter values referenced from
Chapter~\ref{ch:methods} and Chapter~\ref{ch:setup} but left out of the
prose there to keep those chapters focused on mechanism rather than
numbers. Table~\ref{tab:appendix-loop-params} gives the iteration-loop
parameters used for every experiment reported in this thesis
(Section~\ref{sec:setup-procedure}); Table~\ref{tab:appendix-load-profile}
gives the full \texttt{k8s-explore-refine} load-profile parameters
introduced in Section~\ref{sec:methods-load-profile};
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-scenarios} gives the endpoint and functional-test
counts for the seven evaluated scenarios introduced in
Section~\ref{sec:setup-scenarios}.
